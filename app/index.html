    Roadmap to platinum — about 200 hours

    Five chapters, no online trophies, no mission replay and no New Game+. The story is 60–80 hours; the long tail is the 100% checklist. Do the story first, then the checklist, then the cleanup — and keep a save at the start and end of every chapter (15 manual slots plus 1 autosave). Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off.

    Times are PowerPyx's estimates: story 60–80 h, 100% checklist about 100 h, cleanup about 10 h.

    Missables — only one trophy can be missed

    There is no mission replay in GTA VI, so the guide keeps chapter saves. But only one trophy is potentially missable. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story: quest-givers, fences and most collectibles return or stay available, and the 100% checklist counts progress made during the story.

    Things that look missable but are not

    • Optional missions: some disappear after a chapter, and some quest-givers are temporarily unavailable (Kenton returns in Chapters 2–3) — but every trophy quest-giver is available again after the story.
    • Fences: some become permanently unavailable — no trophy depends on them.
    • Magazine collectibles in story buildings: they also spawn elsewhere.
    • Time-sensitive hourglass quests: not trophy-relevant.
    • Criminal Profile: affects police behaviour only. Irredeemable is permanent — avoid it — but it does not block any trophy.
    • Jason / Lucia relationship status: does not matter for trophies.

    Sources & method

    This guide was written on 19 November 2026, the day GTA VI released, so detailed in-game verification is still arriving. Every guide below names its sources; where a step is not yet verified, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing a location.

    Where the facts come from

    • Official trophy names, descriptions and grades — the PSN trophy list, cross-checked on Exophase and PSNProfiles (19 Nov 2026). The two lists agree on all 55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden on PSN.
    • Progress targets (0/100, 0/250, …) — PSNProfiles' trophy counters for the 20 trophies that carry one.
    • Guides, roadmap, missables and counts (chapter and mission names, collectible totals, shop counts, the 200-hour estimate) — PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap (18 Nov 2026).

    What this guide does not do

    • It does not copy text from any source — every guide is written in this guide's own words, and a guide that repeats the official description adds nothing.
    • It does not invent specific locations or steps. Five trophies (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) are still listed as currently unknown by PowerPyx; their guides say so and note only the unconfirmed lead.
    • It contains no Rockstar logo, artwork, box art or game screenshots — the look is original.
    • It makes no network requests. The source links above open only when you click them.
